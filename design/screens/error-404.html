Design spec — notes

Entry motion

Numerals settle with one slow fade+rise. The dice button has the springiest press in the product (tiny wiggle). Reduced motion: static, no wiggle.

Focus on arrival

The headline (h1) receives focus on navigation so screen readers announce the error immediately.

500 independence

The 500 variant renders with zero data dependencies — it must work when everything else failed.
