Design spec — notes

Gestures

Sheet dismisses by handle-drag or scrim tap, with a soft spring settle — always with a visible Cancel equivalent. Reduced motion: fade only.

Focus

Drawer and sheets trap focus; Esc/back closes; focus returns to the opener. Hamburger carries aria-expanded.

RTL

The drawer enters from the end edge in RTL automatically (logical positioning) — verify with the review toggle.
