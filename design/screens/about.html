About Linuxhub

What is Linuxhub

Linuxhub is a catalog of (almost) every Linux distribution — releases, editions, system requirements, and downloads from trusted mirrors, in one place and in your language.

We don't host ISO files. Every download resolves to an official mirror chosen for your region, with the checksum right next to the button.

312 distributions 4.8M downloads served 56 languages

How rankings work

Rankings come from our own signals only: page views, download clicks, and release recency. We deliberately don't scrape third-party popularity charts — the method is simple enough to explain in one sentence, and that's the point.

Where the data comes from

Release and mirror data is fetched from each project's official sources — release APIs, announcement pages, and mirror manifests — on a regular schedule. Every ingested record keeps its source URL and fetch time.

  • Official project APIs and release pages, always preferred
  • Mirror directories like Fedora MirrorManager and Arch mirror status
  • Wikidata for structured metadata, checked weekly

Content & trademarks

Descriptions and guides are written in our own words after reading each project's official documentation, and every page cites its sources. Distro names and logos belong to their projects; logos are used per each project's brand guidelines, with attribution. Spot something wrong? Report it.

Crawler policy

Our fetcher identifies itself as linuxhub-ingest with a link to this section, respects robots.txt, sends conditional requests, and never exceeds one request per second per host. If you run an official distro resource and want different limits, write to crawler@linuxhub.example.
