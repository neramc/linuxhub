Fedora

RPM family Active English fallback

A polished, fast-moving distribution sponsored by Red Hat — the newest stable GNOME, kernel, and toolchains, four weeks after upstream.

Official site

Fedora is where much of the Linux desktop's future ships first. Backed by Red Hat and built by a large community, it delivers a new release roughly every six months with the latest stable GNOME, kernel, and developer toolchains — while staying reliable enough for daily work.

Who it's for

Developers and enthusiasts who want current software without living on a rolling release. If you want this year's GNOME with sensible defaults and SELinux enabled out of the box, Fedora Workstation is the reference experience.

Editions

Workstation (GNOME) is the flagship. KDE Plasma, Xfce, and other desktops ship as official spins, Server and IoT target headless machines, and the Atomic desktops (Silverblue, Kinoite) offer image-based, rollback-friendly systems.
