Header behavior notes

  • Sticky at 64px; on Home it starts transparent over the hero and gains surface + border after ~64px of scroll.
  • Search trigger opens the CommandPalette (see search comp); at tablet width it collapses to an icon button.
  • At <1024px the primary nav collapses into the hamburger drawer (see mobile-nav comp).
  • Theme toggle cross-morphs sun/moon (200ms); the page theme cross-fades (320ms). Reduced motion: instant swap.
  • Skip-link appears on first Tab, before the wordmark.
  • RTL: the whole header mirrors — verify with the review toggle; chevrons and arrows flip via the icon--flip class.
