Header behavior notes

  • Sticky at 64px on the page background with a hairline bottom border.
  • The search entry is a real input (min 240px, max 400px); typing opens the suggestion list; ⌘K focuses it. Below 640px it collapses into the search icon in the compact header.
  • Below 1024px the nav collapses into the hamburger drawer (see mobile-nav comp).
  • Theme toggle swaps sun/moon (200ms); the page cross-fades (320ms). Reduced motion: instant.
  • Skip-link appears on first Tab, before the wordmark.
  • RTL mirrors the whole header; chevrons/arrows flip via icon--flip.
  • The footer stays dark in both themes (Flathub trait).
