Fedora Linux is built by a worldwide community and sponsored by Red Hat. A new version arrives roughly every six months; the current one is Fedora <Latest slug="fedora" />. Fedora is known for adopting new open-source technology early, and what matures here moves on through CentOS Stream into Red Hat Enterprise Linux.

### Highlights

- **New defaults first.** Fedora has repeatedly switched to new defaults ahead of other distributions: Wayland for the GNOME session (Fedora 25), Btrfs as the desktop file system (Fedora 33) and PipeWire for audio (Fedora 34). It is the “First” among the four values the project names as its foundations: Freedom, Friends, Features and First.
- **Desktops as upstream ships them.** The flagship Workstation edition keeps GNOME close to how its developers ship it, with hardly any distribution theming or extensions on top.
- **dnf and Flatpak.** System packages are RPMs managed with `dnf`; since Fedora 41 the rewritten DNF5 is the default. Desktop apps can also come from Flatpak.
- **About 13 months of updates.** Each release is maintained until four weeks after the release two versions later, so you can skip a release and still stay on a supported one.

### Editions and variants

- **Workstation** (GNOME) and **KDE Plasma Desktop** are the official desktop editions, alongside the Server, IoT, Cloud and CoreOS editions.
- **Spins** put another desktop first, such as Xfce, Cinnamon, MATE, LXQt or i3.
- **Atomic desktops** (Silverblue for GNOME, Kinoite for KDE Plasma, and Sway, Budgie and COSMIC Atomic) update the system as one image. A bad update can be rolled back, and apps are installed mostly as Flatpaks.

### Who it suits

- Developers who want a current kernel, desktop and toolchain in a tested release
- People who want GNOME or KDE Plasma the way their developers made them
- Anyone working with RHEL-based servers who wants to learn what is coming next

### Good to know

- Because of patent concerns, some media codecs and proprietary drivers such as NVIDIA’s are not included by default. Add the community repository RPM Fusion if you need them.
- With a little over a year of support, plan on moving to a new version once a year. GNOME Software or `dnf system-upgrade` upgrades the system in place.
- Adopting new technology early means some hardware or third-party software may lag behind right after a release.

### A short history

In 2003 the release Red Hat had been preparing as Red Hat Linux 10 came out as Fedora Core 1, the first Fedora. “Core” was later dropped from the name, which is now Fedora Linux.
