Manjaro delivers Arch's rolling model with training wheels: updates are
held briefly in Manjaro's own testing branches for extra validation,
hardware detection is automatic, and a graphical installer gets you running
in minutes.

You keep `pacman` and access to the AUR without hand-building the system,
and kernels are managed as a first-class setting — multiple versions can be
installed side by side and switched from a control panel.

## Editions

Official editions ship KDE Plasma, GNOME, and Xfce, each with Manjaro's
tooling preconfigured. Community editions cover additional desktops and
window managers.
