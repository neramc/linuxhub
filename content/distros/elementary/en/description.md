elementary OS pairs an Ubuntu base with **Pantheon**, a deliberate,
design-first desktop with its own human interface guidelines — consistent
typography, restrained animation, and apps that do one thing well.

Its **AppCenter** is a curated store where independent developers publish
apps built for the platform, on a pay-what-you-can model that includes
free. The result feels closer to a finished product than a parts kit.

## Editions

There is one edition. Releases track Ubuntu LTS bases and carry mineral
names (Horus, Circe, …); each is supported while its base LTS is.
