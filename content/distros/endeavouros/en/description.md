EndeavourOS is Arch made approachable: a friendly Calamares installer, a
handful of purple-tinted defaults, and a famously welcoming community —
while the installed system stays essentially vanilla Arch underneath.

Unlike heavier Arch derivatives it adds almost no custom tooling: updates
come straight from Arch's repositories, plus a small EndeavourOS repo for
its own utilities. When you ask "is this just Arch?", the answer is
intentionally yes.

## Editions

One ISO, many desktops: the installer offers KDE Plasma (the default
suggestion), GNOME, Xfce, and several window-manager options, installed
fresh from the repos at setup time.
