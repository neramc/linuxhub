Zorin OS is designed for people leaving Windows or macOS: its Appearance
tool switches the whole desktop layout — taskbar, dock, menus — to match
what your hands already know, on an Ubuntu LTS base.

The polish is the product: curated defaults, preinstalled essentials, and
support for running many Windows apps guided by built-in detection that
suggests the Linux-native alternative first.

## Editions

**Core** is the free mainline with the standard layouts. **Lite** targets
old or low-power hardware with a lighter desktop. **Pro** is a paid edition
adding premium layouts and bundled creative software; the base OS is the
same.
